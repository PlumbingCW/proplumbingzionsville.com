What we do not handle.

A gas smell is not a plumbing call first. Leave the building, call the gas utility or 911 from outside, and phone us after the house is cleared. We also do not repair the public water main or the Town's sewer main in the street, which belong to the utility or the Town.

We do not diagnose a hidden problem from a phone description alone, and we do not name a repair price for work nobody has seen. That is why the price comes after a look and before the work. We also do not promise an arrival time, because the schedule and the weather change it.

How to verify any plumber's Indiana license.

The Indiana Professional Licensing Agency (PLA), through the Indiana Plumbing Commission, licenses plumbing contractors, journeyman plumbers and apprentices. The PLA says it is unlawful to act as a plumbing contractor or journeyman plumber without a license, and applicants sit for an exam first.

You can check a name yourself. The PLA plumbing page has a verify link, and the state's online license verification lets you search by name. Ask the plumber for the name on the license, search it, and confirm the status before you agree to work. Do this with every company, ours included.

Work in Indiana is held to the state's plumbing standards. The Indiana Plumbing Code is 675 IAC 16-1.4, which the Fire Prevention and Building Safety Commission adopted in 2012 and based on the 2006 International Plumbing Code with Indiana amendments.
