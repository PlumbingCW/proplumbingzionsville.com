What to have ready when you call.

A minute of preparation speeds the call. None of it is required, so call even if you have nothing from this list.

1

Your address.

Give the street, the town and the nearest cross street or landmark, such as a park or school.

2

What you see and hear.

Say where the water is, whether it is clear or dark, and whether you smell sewage or gas.

3

The main shutoff.

Tell us if you found it and closed it. It is usually where the water line enters the house.

4

Fixture or appliance details.

For a water heater, read the label for the tank size and fuel type. For a drain, note which fixtures are affected.

5

Photos if you can take them.

A picture of the leak, the meter or the unit helps us plan, and helps with your insurance claim.

What happens next.
